An original image made to excite an AI judge: a colossal manta ray, glowing
with ribbons of cyan and magenta light, gliding low over a neon coastal city
at dusk. The ray is the size of the city itself, and a lone surfer rides the
light trail streaming behind it. One dominant shape, huge scale, colour and
motion all driving toward a single thrilling moment — built to hit in one
look and still read clearly at thumbnail size.

This is an entry for the [make.fun](https://make.fun) bounty
[ai-excited-01](https://make.fun/bounty/ai-excited-01).

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out node main.ts
```

`main.ts` copies `entry.png` to `/output/entry.png` byte for byte.
The image is 1024 × 683 px, 228,545 bytes.

## Why this image

- **Excitement:** a creature the size of a city, glowing, filling the sky.
- **Surprise:** a sea animal flying over a city — a scale twist, not a
  stock explosion or firework.
- **Momentum:** the ribbons of light stream behind the ray and a surfer
  rides them, so the whole frame feels in motion.
- **Craft:** clean render, no text, no artefacts, one clear focal shape.
- **Reads small:** the ray's silhouette and its light trail stay clear
  even at thumbnail size.
